MYT PURPLE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

This policy describes Myt Purple’s optional Google account feature and this support website. The application is operated by the Myt Purple maintainer. Support contact: Myt Purple support tracker. This contact channel is public; do not post personal account details.

1. Google sign-in and information accessed

When you choose Google sign-in, Google provides its own account-selection and authorization flow. Myt Purple does not ask you to send a Google password or verification code to the developer.

The registered permissions include your Google account email address, basic profile information and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly. The YouTube permission allows viewing account information that Google exposes through supported YouTube APIs, such as supported channel and playlist data. Availability varies by account and API.

2. How account data is used

Account identity is used to identify the account you selected. Google authorization credentials are used to make authorized account requests to Google’s YouTube Data API and display the resulting account information in the app. This authorization is read-only; it does not grant permission to upload, edit or delete YouTube content.

Local, on-device collections are separate from the Google account feature. Signing out of a Google session does not necessarily delete local collections.

3. Sharing and limited use

Authorized account requests are sent to Google, which processes them under its own policies. Google user data must be used only to provide the account features described here. Myt Purple does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, credit decisions, or training generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Myt Purple’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The support tracker is a contact channel, not an account shared with app users.

4. Session, retention and deletion controls

Use the app’s sign-out control to clear its local Google session. To revoke the app’s authorization at Google, visit Google Account connections and remove Myt Purple’s access. Revocation prevents future authorized requests; it does not delete data held in your own Google account.

You can clear local app data through Android’s app-storage settings, or uninstall the app, to remove its local storage. Google Play services may maintain account information independently of this app. Google retains and deletes Google-hosted information according to its own account controls and policies.

If you contact support through GitHub, the information you choose to post is public and is used to respond to your request. Do not post passwords, verification codes, authorization tokens, account email addresses or sensitive account exports. GitHub processes support activity under its own privacy policies. Use GitHub’s edit/delete controls to remove your own posted information, or request maintainer assistance without reposting sensitive details.

5. Security and third-party services

The account feature uses Google’s authorization mechanism and HTTPS account API requests. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure. Review Google’s Privacy Policy for Google and YouTube processing.

This static support website does not include developer-added analytics, advertising scripts, sign-in forms or tracking cookies. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical request information such as IP addresses under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Visiting a link to a third-party service is subject to that service’s policies.

6. Eligibility and changes

Use Google account features only if you meet Google’s account-eligibility requirements and applicable local requirements. This policy may change with the app’s features. An updated policy will be posted here with its effective date.

7. Contact

For privacy, access, deletion or support requests, use the Myt Purple support tracker. Describe the issue without posting personal or sensitive account information.
