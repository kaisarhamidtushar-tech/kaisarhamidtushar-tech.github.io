Your account.

An Android media application with optional Google sign-in and read-only access to supported YouTube account collections.

How account access works ↗
01

Choose your own Google account

Google handles account selection and consent. Each person signs in with their own account; the developer’s account is not shared with app users.

02

Read-only permission

When you opt in, the app requests access to view supported YouTube account data. This permission does not allow the app to upload, edit or delete your YouTube content.

03

You control access

Use the app’s sign-out control to end its session. You can also revoke the app’s Google access from your Google Account settings.

Account access is optional

Local collections and Google account data are separate. Which collections Google makes available depends on your account and the supported YouTube APIs.

App registration, user eligibility, consent and Google verification rules still apply. This site does not promise that sign-in is already available or that Google has verified the app.

Support

For account-access, privacy or app-support questions, use the Myt Purple support tracker. Support issues are public; do not post personal account information.
